# 素材來源與授權

- `models/bld_rl_l2_2x2_sample.*.glb`：WebCity 原創測試住宅，由 `scripts/generateAssetSample.ts` 生成，含原創立面貼圖；未使用第三方遊戲素材。原始檔保留於 `assets-source/`。
- KTX2 解碼器：Three.js 隨附的 Basis Universal transcoder，來源 [BinomialLLC/basis_universal](https://github.com/BinomialLLC/basis_universal)，Apache-2.0。由 Vite 打包為同站、帶內容雜湊的 JS/WASM，不連接外部 CDN。
- Three.js GLTFLoader / KTX2Loader：MIT，隨 Three.js 套件。
- Meshopt decoder：MIT，來源 [zeux/meshoptimizer](https://github.com/zeux/meshoptimizer)，使用 Three.js 隨附版本。

原始碼中的第三方授權註記由建置工具保留。新增外部模型時須另列作者、原始下載來源、授權版本及修改內容，不能沿用原創標示。

## M3-08 road surfaces

Original deterministic asphalt aggregate and staggered paving texture generated by `src/world/roadDetail.ts` for WebCity. No external images or commercial game assets used. Project-owned original work.

## M3-09 vegetation

Original six tree and two shrub geometries, plus a procedurally authored silhouette atlas in `src/render/vegetation/`. Created for WebCity; no external textures or commercial game assets. Project-owned original work.

## M3-10 residential catalog

60 RL and 30 RH original architectural assets, authored parametrically for WebCity in `catalogGeometry.ts` and exported by `scripts/generateModels.ts`. Original facade and emission atlases are generated by `scripts/modelAtlas.ts`. No external or commercial-game models or textures. Recipes and three LOD budgets: `assets-source/catalog/res-recipes.json`. Project-owned original work.

## M3-11 商業、工業與辦公

99 款 CL／CH／I／O 模型及四組 atlas 為 WebCity 原創參數化離線素材，沿用專案授權，無外部遊戲素材。來源與重建方式：`assets-source/catalog/cio-recipes.json`、`scripts/generateModels.ts`、`scripts/modelAtlas.ts`、`src/render/buildings/catalogGeometry.ts`；輸出為 `public/assets/models/bld_{cl,ch,i,o}_*.glb`，授權 metadata 見 manifest。

## M3-12 公共設施與地標

30 種設施及 12 種地標 GLB／civic atlas 為 WebCity 原創離線參數化素材，沿用專案授權，未採用外部商業遊戲素材。來源：`src/render/facilities/geometry.ts`、`scripts/generateFacilities.ts`、`scripts/modelAtlas.ts`；原始檔與重建紀錄位於 `assets-source/catalog/`。地標為一般建築形式的原創組合，非特定商業遊戲資產或現存地標之重製。
